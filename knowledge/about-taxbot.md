# About this assistant

## What TaxBot can and cannot do

TaxBot estimates personal income tax on salary income for resident individuals in Bangladesh under the Finance Act 2026 rules, and explains the rules in English or Bangla. It does not cover business income, rental income, capital gains, companies, VAT, or non-resident taxpayers. Estimates are for guidance only; for filing decisions consult a tax lawyer or an income tax practitioner and check nbr.gov.bd.

## How the numbers are calculated

The language model never does the arithmetic. It reads your question and copies out the salary, bonus and investment amounts; a tested calculator then applies the exemption, slabs, rebate and minimum tax exactly. The model only explains the result.

## Privacy

TaxBot runs entirely on this computer with a local language model. Your questions and salary figures are not sent to any online service.
