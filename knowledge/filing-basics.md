# Filing your income tax return in Bangladesh

## Who needs a TIN (টিআইএন)

A TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number) is your income tax registration number. You get an e-TIN online from the National Board of Revenue (NBR) using your NID. Salaried people whose income is above the tax-free threshold, and anyone who needs a Proof of Submission of Return (PSR) for services such as some loans, credit cards, trade licences or company directorships, should have a TIN and file a return.

## Return filing deadline (রিটার্ন জমার শেষ তারিখ)

For individuals the normal deadline, called Tax Day (কর দিবস), is 30 November after the end of the income year (the income year runs 1 July to 30 June). NBR sometimes extends it, so check the current notice on nbr.gov.bd. Filing late can mean extra charges and may cost you the investment rebate for that year.

## How to file (কীভাবে রিটার্ন জমা দেবেন)

Most salaried individuals can file online through NBR's e-return system using their TIN, mobile number and NID. You will need your annual salary statement from your employer, the certificate of tax deducted at source (TDS) from your salary, bank statements and proof of any eligible investments (DPS, savings certificates, provident fund, shares). After submitting you can download the acknowledgement (PSR).

## Tax deducted from salary (বেতন থেকে উৎসে কর কর্তন)

Employers estimate your yearly tax and deduct a share from each month's salary, then deposit it with the government. That deduction is an advance payment, not the final tax: when you file the return you compare your real tax with what was deducted. If too much was deducted you can claim the difference; if too little, you pay the balance.

## Wealth statement (সম্পদ বিবরণী)

Along with the return, individuals submit a statement of assets and liabilities and, above certain limits, a statement of living expenses. Keep records of bank balances, savings certificates, land, vehicles and loans.
